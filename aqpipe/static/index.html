    From space to street

    India has about 500 ground monitors, mostly in big cities. ISRO's INSAT-3DR sees the whole country every half hour. This map turns what the satellite sees into the PM2.5 you breathe.

    1. Satellite — aerosol optical depth: how much sunlight the whole air column blocks. Gaps are clouds or bright desert.
    2. Corrected — the same haze divided by mixing height and humidity swelling: a low ceiling traps it near the ground.
    3. PM2.5 — a model trained on CPCB stations turns satellite + weather into µg/m³, coloured by India's AQI bands.
    4. CAMS — the European forecast, with no INSAT input, on the same scale.
    5. Δ INSAT — the model with the satellite minus without it. Red: the satellite says the air is dirtier.
